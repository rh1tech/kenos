Kenos

Safari’s engine.
Not Safari’s chrome.

A small browser for macOS: the WebKit already on your Mac, tabs and an address field, and almost nothing else in the way.

Inside the glovebox

What you actually press.

Shield

Ads stop before they load

EasyList, EasyPrivacy and uBlock’s own filters as WebKit rules. Pause a host when a page breaks; update the lists when you want.

Settings › Shield
Passwords

Keychain only

Offer to save, autofill, Touch ID to reveal. Nothing is uploaded to us — the items live in the macOS keychain under Kenos.

⇧⌘L
Reading

Strip the page, keep the words

Reading mode, hide any element, float the video when you leave the tab. The shortcuts stay out of the chrome until you need them.

⇧⌘R · ⇧⌘H · ⇧⌘P
Tabs

Top, bottom, or down the side

A strip or a column you can tuck away. Spaces if you want separate sets of tabs. Sleep idle ones without losing your place.

⇧⌘S

On this Mac

Browsing stays here.

No Kenos account. No analytics package of ours. History and bookmarks sit in Application Support; passwords in the keychain. The only calls Kenos makes for itself are update checks and optional filter-list refreshes — written down in the privacy policy.
